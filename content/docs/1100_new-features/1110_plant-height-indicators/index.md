Plant height indicators help you compare the heights of different plants at a glance!

Use plant height indicators along with your garden’s [compass orientation](https://info.planter.garden/new-features/garden-compass/) to plan for shade in your garden; either to avoid shading out smaller plants or to intentionally shade them to help keep them cool!

Plant height indicators are based on a range of average plant heights, categorized as **short, medium, tall, and extra-tall**. They do not necessarily reflect the absolute shortest and tallest varieties available. Plant heights can also vary depending on your trellising system and pruning.

To set your own custom plant heights you can [edit the plant info](https://info.planter.garden/plant-information/customize-plants/#customize-plant-care-information). The indicator will change if the custom height bumps the plant into a different height category.

1. **Open the Garden Layers Menu**

   {{< screenshot 01-open-the-garden-layers-menu.webp "Open the Garden Layers Menu" >}}

   In the garden view, tap the layers menu in the upper-left of the screen.
2. **Enable Plant Height Indicators**

   {{< screenshot 02-enable-plant-height-indicators.webp "Enable Plant Height Indicators" >}}

   In the drop-down menu, tap the 'Heights' option to enable plant height indicators in the garden.

   Note: Plant height indicators can be toggled on-or-off per garden.
3. **Disable Plant Height Indicators**

   {{< screenshot 03-disable-plant-height-indicators.webp "Disable Plant Height Indicators" >}}

   Repeat the steps above to disable the plant height indicators.
