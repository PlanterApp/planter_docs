Now it’s easier than ever to multi-select garden squares. Simply tap two opposing corners and all squares in between will be selected!

Note: Corner multi-select only works for rectangular areas.

{{< video mp4-src="recording.mp4" attributes="controls muted playsinline preload=metadata" >}}

1. **Activate the Corner Selector**

   {{< screenshot 01-activate-the-corner-selector.webp "Activate the Corner Selector" >}}

   Tap the corner selector button (third option from the right).

2. **Select the Top-Left Corner**

   {{< screenshot 02-select-first-corner-of-bed.webp "Select First Corner of Bed" >}}

   Tap the top-left corner of the desired square to initiate the selection of the planting area.

3. **Select the Bottom-Right Corner**

   {{< screenshot 03-select-opposite-corner-of-bed.webp "Select Opposite Corner of Bed" >}}

   Tap the bottom-right corner of an opposing square to complete the multi-square selection.

   Once the squares are selected, you can add plants, add notes, or compost existing plants.
